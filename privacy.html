Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy covers the @humzabuilds Instagram account, this website, and the automated direct messages that send free Claude skills. They are operated by Merchant Matrix AI LLC, Windermere, Florida.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram username and the account ID Instagram gives us, when you comment on a @humzabuilds post or send @humzabuilds a direct message.
  • The text of that comment or message, so we can tell which free skill you asked for.
  • Whether you follow @humzabuilds, checked through Instagram at the moment we send a skill.
  • The email address you type into the chat, if you choose to give it.
  • The email address you type into the "Get the next one free" form on this website, if you choose to give it, plus which page you signed up on and when. We do not store your IP address with it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your comment with a private message and send you the skill you asked for.
  • To email you new free skills and occasional updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To email you the next free guide when you sign up on this website. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We never sell your data and never share it with anyone except the services that run this, which are Meta (Instagram), Cloudflare (which stores website signups) and our email provider.

How long we keep it

We keep your username, messages and email until you ask us to delete them or unsubscribe. If you unsubscribe, we keep only your email address marked as unsubscribed so we never email you again. Ask us and we delete it. Message history older than 12 months is deleted.

Delete your data

Send the word DELETE to @humzabuilds on Instagram, or email humzabuilds@gmail.com. We delete everything we hold about you within 7 days and reply to confirm. Details are on the data deletion page.
