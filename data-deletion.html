Delete your data

Last updated October 21, 2026

You can have everything @humzabuilds holds about you deleted at any time: your Instagram username and ID, your comments and messages to us, and your email address.

How

  • Send the word DELETE in a direct message to @humzabuilds on Instagram, or
  • Email humzabuilds@gmail.com with your Instagram username and the subject "Delete my data".

What happens next

We delete your records within 7 days and send you a message confirming it. Removing this app's access in your Instagram settings also stops any further messages from us.
